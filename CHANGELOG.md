# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-18

First release of laravel-quebec-incidents: the confidentiality incident register (Quebec Law 25, s. 3.8) kept 5 years, the risk of serious injury assessment (s. 3.7), and the notices to the CAI and the persons concerned (s. 3.5), with public notices, deferrals during an investigation and an overdue check. Part of the Quebec Law 25 suite for Laravel.
